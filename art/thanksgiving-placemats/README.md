# The Thanksgiving Placemats (Bud, 26 Oct 2026)

Opened 25 Oct 2026 (Alyx: "Open a new category for both christmas flyers
and thanksgiving flyers respectively. Placemats."). Flat artwork, as Bud
sent it, untouched. The occasion page's placemat is the Neoprene,
12 x 18 in, printed 5610 x 3839 (1.46 : 1, `lib/products-catalog.js`);
tools/premade-fit.py's `placemats` category makes `print/<key>.jpg` and
`show/<key>.jpg` from each. Placemats are drawn at 1536 x 1024 (3 : 2),
which the fit trims by about 1% a side. A placemat sits under a plate,
so the picture and the words keep to the top, the sides and the bottom,
and the middle stays calm.
