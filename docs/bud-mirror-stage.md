# Bud prompt — the Mirror Mirror stage (background band)

A static painted backdrop for the 14oz handle cup and the coffee mug, which
share the same wrap. The mirror and the figure are composited on top as a
cut-out, so this painting holds no mirror and no people — it is the room only.

**Deliver at exactly 1995 x 930 pixels.**

---

A richly painted storybook oil painting of an EMPTY candlelit chamber, in a
wide panoramic format, 1995 x 930 pixels.

THERE ARE NO PEOPLE IN THIS PICTURE AND NO MIRROR. It is an empty room,
waiting. Do not paint a figure, a face, a portrait, a mirror, a reflection or
any framed glass anywhere in it.

The room: an old stone chamber dressed for a grand evening. Tall stone walls
hung with deep patterned tapestry in burgundy and midnight blue. A branched
candelabra with lit candles standing at the LEFT, its warm golden light falling
across the room from left to right. Heavy velvet drapery gathered at the far
left. Toward the right, a low table with a crystal decanter, a few old
leather-bound books, and a vase of white roses. Worn flagstone floor.

LIGHT: one consistent source, the candles at the LEFT. Everything is lit warmly
from the left and falls into deeper shadow toward the right. Keep this rule
absolutely consistent across the whole width — it is the single most important
thing in this painting.

THE MIDDLE OF THE PICTURE MUST STAY SIMPLE. Across the central third, keep the
wall plain and the shadow deep — quiet tapestry and stonework, nothing detailed,
nothing bright, no strong shapes and no objects. Something tall will be placed
there later and must read clearly against it. Put the interesting furniture and
props to the left and right of that central third.

THE TWO ENDS MUST MEET. The far LEFT edge and the far RIGHT edge of this
painting join each other when it wraps around a cup, so they must continue into
one another seamlessly — the same wall, the same tone, the same depth of shadow,
the same height of floor and ceiling line at both edges. Let both ends settle
into quiet, unremarkable shadow so the join is invisible. Nothing important, no
object and no strong edge, within the last tenth of the picture at either end.

Fill the entire canvas edge to edge. No border, no frame, no matte, no vignette,
no black bars, no panels or divisions of any kind. One single continuous room
from the far left to the far right.

Palette: deep golds, warm amber candlelight, burgundy and midnight blue in
shadow. Painterly oil technique with visible brushwork, storybook illustration
feel, romantic and slightly magical. Warm and inviting, never cold, never
modern, not photographic.

---

## What it is for

The composite is the same one Needles' studio already uses: a static painted
stage with a cut-out placed on it. Here the stage is this room; the actor is the
mirror with the customer's face in its glass, returned on magenta by the merge
and keyed out with `despillMagenta`, then set down with `placeFigure` and
`drawContactShadow`.

Three things in the prompt exist only to serve that composite, and they are the
three to check when the art comes back:

- **light from the LEFT, consistently.** The cut-out's prompt will be written to
  match this painting, which is only possible because the painting is fixed. A
  backdrop that changes its mind about where the candles are makes every overlay
  sit wrong.
- **a quiet middle third.** The mirror lands there. Detail or brightness behind
  it fights it.
- **ends that meet, and nothing important in the last tenth of either.** The
  join lands behind the handle on the 14oz cup, which is forgiving, but the
  coffee mug shares this band and is not.

## Correction: there is no second face

I reported a second carved face in the cartouche at the foot of the frame and
said the device needed one face, not two. Alyx went looking for it, could not
find it at any magnification, and asked me to check. He was right.

At 3x there is no face there. It is acanthus scrollwork around a central
palmette, and at thumbnail size the bulb with the leaves fanning beneath it
reads as a head with hair. I had seen it small and not verified before calling
it a flaw.

The crest is the only face in the frame, which is what the device requires.
Nothing to paint out.
