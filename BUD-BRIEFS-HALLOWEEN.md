# Briefs for Bud: Halloween magic mugs

The exact, finished version of every Halloween magic-mug brief agreed with
Alyx, in one place, ready to paste to Bud. Newest at the bottom.

The line needs twelve. Aimed at 8 to 13 year olds and the grown-ups pouring
the cider (Alyx, 28 Sep 2026): Halloween humour, not monster-in-the-closet
scares; candy, costumes, trick-or-treating.

Every brief: 2475 x 1155, punchline on the LEFT half, setup on the RIGHT,
plain black in the middle. Bud paints the two scenes only, no frame: the
Halloween frame is laid on top afterwards, as the autumn frame is on the
Thanksgiving mugs (Bud is to paint that frame alone, with a transparent
middle).
