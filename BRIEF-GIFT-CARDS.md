# Gift card designs — brief for Bud (from Claude, 24 Sep 2026, revised)

Revised the same day: the first draft tied every card to Muggsy. Alyx: "He
shouldn't have had to stick with Muggsy, why couldn't he do one of Needles,
of Sly, of Chipper?" The cast is open; the card's physical facts are not.

## The set
Five cards, one per occasion: EVERYDAY, HAPPY BIRTHDAY, THANK YOU, HAPPY
HOLIDAYS, CONGRATULATIONS. Any of the crew can front a card — Muggsy,
Needles, Sly, Chipper — one character per card, Bud's pick of who suits
which occasion, of pose and of scene. What ties them together is the house
style of the Muggsy card (`art/gift-card/muggshotz-gift-card-50.png`): the
MUGGSHOTZ logo lock-up, the brushy white-and-orange lettering, the black
splatter ground, and the attitude — mischievous, never cute for its own sake.

## The one rule: no amount on the art
Every card is delivered with NO dollar figure. The site adds the amount
($25 / $50 / $100) as its own layer, so one design serves every value. A
clean patch of background bottom right, under GIFT CARD, about the size the
"$50" took, is left for the number.

## Format (every card)
- Credit-card proportions, landscape: 1581 x 995 px minimum (1.588:1).
  Bigger at the same ratio is fine.
- PNG, sRGB, no transparency, square corners.
- Lettering and faces at least 60 px in from every edge.
- Front only. Square prints the back.

## On every card
The MUGGSHOTZ logo top right; the words GIFT CARD, same size and place; the
occasion line in the brush lettering where "Consider yourself mugged." sits
(EVERYDAY keeps that line); the icon row MUGS · SHIRTS · TUMBLERS ·
MOUSEPADS · AND MORE!

## First, on its own
EVERYDAY: the Muggsy card with the "$50" removed and the background carried
through. It goes on plastic, so it comes first. A different face on the
plastic is Bud's to propose before drawing.
