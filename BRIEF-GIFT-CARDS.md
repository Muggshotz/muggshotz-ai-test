# Gift card designs — brief for Bud (from Claude, 24 Sep 2026)

## What we have
The Muggsy card (raccoon, shades, orange hoodie, pointing, mug in paw;
"MUGGSHOTZ — Consider yourself mugged." / "GIFT CARD" / "$50"; icon row; red
bow) is the master: `art/gift-card/muggshotz-gift-card-50.png`. Everything
below keeps its DNA: same Muggsy, same logo lock-up, same brushy
white-and-orange type, same black splatter ground.

## The one rule that changes: no amount on the art
Every card is delivered with NO dollar figure. The amount ($25 / $50 / $100)
is added by the site as its own layer, so one design serves every value.
The area where "$50" sat (bottom right, under GIFT CARD) is clean background
so a number can drop in. "GIFT CARD" stays where it is.

## Format (every design)
- Credit-card proportions, landscape: 1581 x 995 px minimum (the exact card
  ratio, 1.588:1). Bigger is fine at the same ratio.
- PNG, sRGB, no transparency, no rounded corners baked in.
- All lettering and Muggsy's face at least 60 px in from every edge; the
  outer 60 px can be trimmed on the physical card.
- Front only. Square prints the back.

## Deliverables, in this order
1. **Everyday** — the existing card with the "$50" removed and the background
   carried through. Nothing else changes. This one is printed on plastic.
2. **Happy Birthday** — crooked party hat, a cake with one candle, confetti
   among the splatter, bow stays. Headline "HAPPY BIRTHDAY" where "Consider
   yourself mugged." sits.
3. **Thank You** — shades tipped down, wink, thumbs-up, mug in the other paw.
   Headline "THANK YOU". Calmer background, more of the orange glow.
4. **Happy Holidays** — Santa hat, mug steaming, snowflakes among the
   splatter, red-and-gold bow. Headline "HAPPY HOLIDAYS" (one card for the
   season).
5. **Congratulations** — tiny paper crown or a medal, both paws up, mug held
   high like a trophy. Headline "CONGRATULATIONS!" Gold sparkle accents.

## Stays the same on all five
The MUGGSHOTZ logo top right; "GIFT CARD" in the same size and place; the
icon row (MUGS · SHIRTS · TUMBLERS · MOUSEPADS · AND MORE!); the MUGGSY patch;
Muggsy's expression: mischievous, never cute for its own sake.

## Naming
`muggshotz-gift-card-everyday.png`, `-birthday.png`, `-thankyou.png`,
`-holidays.png`, `-congratulations.png`. Everyday first, on its own: the
physical card and the Square print order need only that one; the other four
are digital for now.
